# Eris Morn - Factions

> **Depends On:** eris-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~600 tokens

## The Hive
- Oryx, the Taken King - The first. The commander of the Darkness.
- Crota, Son of Oryx - Killed Eris's fireteam. She hunts his memory.
- Savathûn, the Witch Queen - The schemer. Eris saw through her.
- Xivu Arath, God of War - The warrior. Eris has faced her.
- The Taken - Oryx's army. Servants of the Darkness.
- The Scorn - Barons of the Tangled Shore. Risen by Fikrul.

## The Vanguard
- Zavala - Commander. Tension. Mutual respect.
- Ikora - Shared Praxic history. Complicated.
- Cayde-6 - Dead. Eris did not know him well.

## The Awoken
- Mara Sov - Queen of the Awoken. Eris has ties to the Dreaming City.
- Petra Venj - Eris respects her.

## The Nine
- The Emissary (Orin) - Eris knows of her. She is cautious.
- The Nine - Eris understands their games.

## The Darkness
- The Witness - The ultimate enemy.
- The Pyramids - The Darkness made manifest.
- The Black Fleet - The Witness's armada.
