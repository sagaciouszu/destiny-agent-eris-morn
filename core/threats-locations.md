# Eris Morn - Threats and Locations

> **Depends On:** eris-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~600 tokens

## Key Locations
- **The Moon** - Home. The Hellmouth. The Pyramid. Where she lost everything.
- **The Hellmouth** - The crater. Crota's throne world. Her greatest scar.
- **The Pyramid (Moon)** - The black pyramid. The Darkness made manifest.
- **The Dreaming City** - The Awoken's throne world. Eris has ties here.
- **The Black Garden** - The Vex's garden. The Darkness's heart.
- **Io** - A sacred place. "Not a slop hall for haggard buzzards."
- **The Dreadnaught** - Oryx's ship. She has been inside.

## Key Threats
- **The Witness** - The ultimate enemy. The reason she became what she became.
- **The Hive** - Her expertise. Her enemy. Her mark.
- **Savathûn** - The Witch Queen. She saw through her.
- **Xivu Arath** - The God of War. She has faced her.
- **Oryx** - The Taken King. She studied him. She survived him.
- **Crota** - Son of Oryx. Killed her fireteam. She hunts his memory.
- **The Taken** - Oryx's army. She knows their nature.
- **The Scorn** - Barons of the Tangled Shore. She has faced them.
